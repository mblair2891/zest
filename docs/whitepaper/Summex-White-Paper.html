1. Executive summary

Summex is a hospitality operating system for restaurants, bars, and host venues where more than one operator can appear on a single guest check. It is powered by Quantum Reach. Guest-facing cards run exclusively through Quantum Payments.

The product combines service (floor, sections, kitchen and bar routing), SaaS (intake, snapshot quote, contract, guided onboarding), and money movement (one host capture, internal operator allocations, period settlement, a first-party system ledger).

The guest pays once, under the host name. Operators are paid from merchandise share on a period ledger — not from a second terminal at the table.

This paper describes how the system works today. Items that are not built are marked Roadmap. No processor rates are guaranteed. No bank partners are named. No compliance seals are claimed.

2. The problem

Hospitality software still treats a dining room like a retail checkout, and a food hall like several unrelated shops.

  • Fragmented POS. Floor, kitchen, and bar live in different tools. Managers close the night in a spreadsheet.
  • Host + operator venues. The guest is asked to pay twice. Tips, comps, and disputes cannot be split cleanly.
  • Multiple payment brands. Stall operators bring their own processors. The house loses the receipt and any fair cut.
  • Settlement after the fact. A $35 dispute fee, when it exists, is not allocated by who actually sold the food.

Summex’s answer is one system for service and money movement.

3. Platform overview

Summex is a single application. Merchants sign in at summex.app. Organization and location are chosen after authentication. Software billing is separate from guest card processing. Gift cards stay first-party. Integrations never offer Stripe, Square, or similar as a POS processor.

4. Operating models

Single-operator

One brand owns the location. Cards still run on Quantum Payments. The ledger still records captures, tips, cash, voids, and (if filed) disputes.

Host + multiple operators

Host Venue owns the floor, the guest-facing brand, and the card MID. Operator A and Operator B own lines, tickets, and period payouts. They are not card processors. The guest sees one check and one charge.

5. Guest experience

  • One check, even when food and drink belong to different operators.
  • One guest-facing brand (the location / host name).
  • One card capture on Quantum Payments.
  • Printed menu prices stay clean (e.g. $15.00). Cash discount, if posted, is computed — not a second ugly menu.

The guest does not see operator splits, host cut, or ledger rows.

6. Quantum Payments — host capture and internal split

Card tender is a single capture on the host MID. Operators never receive a guest PAN and never onboard as processors inside Summex.

After capture, Summex records a host capture, then allocations to operators by merchandise share (pre-tax product; voids/comps excluded). Tax and service remain with the host unless configured otherwise. Tips default to the house unless pooled.

This is not a live processor split-payout. It is a product-owned ledger. Electronic payout rows address an operator’s account placeholder. Live ACH is not claimed. Marking a period paid records that money was sent outside Summex.

Roadmap: connecting a live bank rail. Payout last4 in the product is a stub for operations rehearsal.

7. Cash discount model

Printed / card price is the source of truth. When cash discount is enabled: cashRaw = printed × (1 − percent/100), then round up to the increment ($0.25, $0.50, $0.75, or $1.00). Exact multiples stay.

Printed / cardRaw 5%Cash (round up $0.25)
$15.00$14.25$14.25
$12.00$11.40$11.50
$7.00$6.65$6.75

Quantum Payments still captures the printed amount. The house is responsible for local cash-discount posting rules.

8. Fees and disputes

Software fees are quoted in intake — not card-processing rates. Card fee % on settlement is a house-configured estimate on the period ledger, not a guaranteed Quantum Payments schedule.

Quantum Payments charges $35.00 only when a real dispute is filed on a closed check with a card capture. Mixed merchandise (e.g. $65 food / $35 drink) splits the fee 65/35 → $22.75 / $12.25. No dispute → $0. Won or lost does not reverse the $35.

9. Security, roles, and audit

PIN access level controls POS tools. Account membership controls the control plane. Platform Admin is not a restaurant owner. The system ledger is append-only with idempotent writes. This paper does not claim PCI DSS, SOC, or other seals. Summex does not store full PAN on guest profiles.

10. Implementation path

  1. Intake — describe the operation. Snapshot quote.
  2. Accept — merchant accepts the quote.
  3. Contract signed — Platform Admin records the commercial step.
  4. Onboarding wizard — org, locations, operators, floor, menu, devices, invites, settlement, go-live.
  5. Live — Open POS. Enable cash discount if the house posts one.

Roadmap (not in this build): live ACH/payout rails, QuickBooks sync, PCI audit letter, guaranteed processor rates.
